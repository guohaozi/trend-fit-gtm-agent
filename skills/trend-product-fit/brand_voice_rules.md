# Brand Voice Rules — voice guidance & words logic

This drives sections 7 (brand voice guidance) and 8 (words to use / avoid) of the GTM
Brief. Voice is not decoration — it's how the brand rides a trend without triggering
the risks from [`risk_taxonomy.md`](risk_taxonomy.md).

---

## The method (4 steps)

1. **Start from positioning, not the trend.** The trend is borrowed energy; the voice
   must still sound like the brand. If the trend's native voice contradicts the brand
   (e.g. a calm wellness brand on a chaotic shock trend), that's a fit problem.
2. **Translate each identified risk into a word ban.** Every risk in section 6 should
   produce 1–3 entries in "words to avoid". This is the link that makes the brief
   coherent: risks → banned words.
3. **Build "words to use" from the safe bridge.** These are the words that land the
   campaign angle *without* stepping on a risk — concrete, benefit-led, honest.
4. **Match register to platform + audience.** TikTok/Reels = casual, fast, first-person.
   LinkedIn/X B2B = plain, credible, low-hype. Never paste corporate copy into a UGC
   format.

---

## Voice tone by product segment (starting points, then adapt)

| Segment | Voice should feel… | Avoid sounding… |
|---------|--------------------|-----------------|
| Apparel / fashion | Tasteful, confident, practical, effortless | Arrogant, classist, salesy |
| Robotics / smart home | Warm, trustworthy, practical, lightly futuristic | Creepy, over-promising, sci-fi |
| AI / creative tools | Creative, fast, maker-oriented, honest | Magical, deceptive, "AI will do everything" |
| B2B SaaS | Plain, credible, specific, peer-to-peer | Hypey, jargon-stuffed, try-hard trendy |
| Beauty / wellness | Caring, inclusive, evidence-aware | Body-shaming, miracle-claiming |

---

## Words-to-use / words-to-avoid logic

**Words to use** should be:
- Concrete and benefit-led ("clean fit", "before-after", "daily routine").
- True — you can stand behind every one.
- On-brand register (match the segment row above).

**Words to avoid** should each trace to a specific risk:

| If the risk is… | Avoid words like… |
|------------------|-------------------|
| Classism / wealth-signalling | rich, elite, old money (as identity), "poor man's X", flex |
| Over-promising (HW/AI) | fully autonomous, replaces humans, perfect, effortless, guaranteed |
| Privacy / surveillance | always watching, knows everything, tracks you |
| Deception / fake identity | fake your face, look like someone else, undetectable |
| Health/body | lose weight, fix your body, flaws, transformation (as a promise) |
| Cringe / forced (B2B) | slang the brand can't carry, "we're so random", forced meme-speak |
| Cultural erasure | presenting a borrowed aesthetic as "just clean/universal" |

---

## Quick examples (segment → use / avoid)

**Apparel × quiet luxury**
Use: clean, polished, timeless, premium-looking, effortless, everyday.
Avoid: rich, fake luxury, elite, old-money cosplay, "poor man's luxury".

**Robotics × smart-home**
Use: helpful, reliable, daily routine, family-friendly, simple setup, you stay in control.
Avoid: replace humans, fully autonomous life, surveillance, sci-fi butler, set-and-forget.

**AI photo tool × before/after**
Use: before-after, creator workflow, product-ready, edit faster, visual upgrade.
Avoid: perfect face, fake identity, unrealistic transformation, effortless wealth.

**B2B SaaS × a relevant builder trend**
Use: how we actually do X, here's the real workflow, what broke, what we fixed.
Avoid: forced slang, "going viral", hype adjectives, anything you'd never say in a sales call.

---

## Final check
Read the "words to use" and "words to avoid" lists side by side with the risk section.
If a banned word has no matching risk, or a risk has no banned word, the brief is
incoherent — fix it before shipping.
